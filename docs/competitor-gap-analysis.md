# QRix — competitor gap analysis

Honest comparison against the incumbents that hold the SERPs QRix wants. **No
fabricated metrics.** Domain Authority, backlink counts and exact traffic are
marked `unmeasured` — they need Moz/Ahrefs/SimilarWeb and were not available here.
What *is* recorded is directly observable: who ranks (from the 3 Sep WebSearch runs
in `aeo-benchmark.md`), and the product facts.

---

## The landscape (observed)

| Competitor | Owns | Signup | Upload model | QRix's honest edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| iLovePDF / iLoveIMG | PDF + image head terms | soft (limits without) | server upload | in-browser tools that never upload |
| Smallpdf | PDF head terms | account-pushed | server upload | free + no account for core tools |
| TinyPNG | image compression | none | server upload | compression runs in the browser |
| remove.bg | background removal | account/credits | server upload | free, in-browser, no credit meter |
| QRCode Monkey / me-qr / tinyqr | "no signup" QR | none | n/a | 34 QR types + real dynamic QR + analytics |
| luxa / snapany / snapwc | VK/social downloaders | none | server | breadth of platforms, AI-referral presence |

DA / backlinks / traffic for every row: **unmeasured** — fill from an SEO tool.

## The gaps QRix can genuinely own

Not "beat iLovePDF at compress PDF" — that is a backlink-authority war. The winnable
gaps are where QRix's product is structurally different and the incumbents cannot
honestly make the same claim:

1. **"Without uploading" / "in your browser."** QRix's device-side tools
   (background removal, OCR, compression, HEIC) run locally. iLoveIMG, TinyPNG,
   remove.bg all upload. This is a true, quotable differentiator and the single
   biggest content wedge. Every Tier-A page in `keyword-priority-map.md` sits here.

2. **One toolbox, many jobs.** The head-term competitors are single-purpose
   (TinyPNG = compression, remove.bg = background). QRix spans QR + PDF + image +
   video + AI + 3D + downloader. Use-case pages ("everything a photographer needs")
   exploit breadth the specialists cannot match. QRix already has 225 use-case URLs.

3. **QR depth.** 34 QR types, dynamic QR with analytics, no expiry, no watermark.
   The "no signup QR" incumbents are thin generators. QRix out-features them; it
   needs the authority to be found.

4. **Russian-sphere downloaders.** VK/OK.ru/Rutube are far less contested than the
   YouTube grabbers QRix (correctly) refuses to build. QRix already earns AI-referral
   traffic here — the gap is Google presence, closable with a few directory links.

## What NOT to do (rules)

- Do not claim QRix is "faster" or "better" than a named competitor without a
  measurement to cite. Comparison pages must be objective tables of verifiable
  facts (free limits, signup, watermark, upload, formats).
- Do not chase the head terms (compress PDF, background remover online) with more
  content. Those are lost without backlinks; effort there is wasted.
