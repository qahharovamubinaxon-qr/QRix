average, not an estimate. Nothing here is inferred from a keyword tool.

One query — the brand, `qrix` — supplies 18 of the 35 clicks at position 3.2.
**Every other query on the site sits between position 36 and 141.**

## The finding that changes the plan

A standard SEO plan says: *take the keywords on page 2–3 (positions 11–30) to
page 1 first, they are the cheapest wins.* Checked against the real data:

> **QRix has no keywords at positions 11–30. Not one.**

The gap between the brand term (3.2) and the next-best non-brand term (36.0) is
the whole story. So the usual "push page 2 to page 1" programme has nothing to
act on, and any plan built on it would be fiction.

## Where the impressions are — and why they are a trap

| Query | Impr. | Position |
|---|---|---|
| remove background | 133 | 90.8 |
| background remover | 114 | 88.2 |
| remove background online | 95 | 87.6 |
| background remover online | 77 | 88.5 |
| remove background online free | 75 | 90.5 |
| bg remover | 74 | 83.8 |
| free background remover | 72 | 95.8 |
| background remover free | 71 | 96.7 |
| *(≈20 more variants)* | ~600 | 85–97 |

The background-remover cluster is **~1,100 impressions — the largest on the
site — and it has produced 1 click in two months.** Position 85–97 means Google
is listing the page somewhere on page 9 or 10, i.e. it knows the page exists and
does not consider the site a credible answer. The competitors here are
remove.bg, Canva and Adobe Express. This is not an on-page problem and no amount
of copywriting moves it; it is a domain-authority problem.

**Do not spend the next month rewriting the background-remover page.**

## The flagship the brief assumed

`pdf to word` — and every variant of it — **does not appear anywhere in the top
1,000 queries.** Zero impressions. Google is not showing `/pdf-tools/pdf-to-word`
for its own name, so optimising that page targets demand the site does not yet
receive. It stays a good page; it is not the lever.

is the one QRix actually serves.

## Priority, from this data

1. **`/bulk-qr`** — best reachable Google target (45 + 10 impressions at 68–70),
   and it was the site's thinnest page. Rebuilt 13 Sep. *(Done.)*
2. **`/qr-tools/telegram`, `/qr-tools/appstore`** — positions 36 and 43, already
   1,800-word pages with FAQ and schema. On-page is not what is holding them
   back; they need external citations.
3. **Content gap: a contactless-menu QR page.** Position 49.5 with 17
   impressions and *no dedicated page* — the query is being served by something
   generic. The one genuinely missing page worth building.
4. **CIS/Yandex cluster** — passport photo 413×531 and OK.ru/VK. Proven clicks.
5. **Everything else waits on off-site authority.**

## What this map deliberately does not claim

No search volumes, no difficulty scores, no competitor domain-authority
figures — this environment has no keyword-data API, and inventing those numbers
would make the document worse, not better. Positions and impressions are real
and dated; treat anything else as unmeasured.
