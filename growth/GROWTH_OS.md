## The engine (what actually produces traffic)

1. **Programmatic SEO** — thousands of intent-exact landing pages that all
   embed a REAL working tool (never thin): converter pairs, resize sizes,
   platform downloader pages, QR types, use-cases. Formula per page: unique
   title/desc/h1/intro (≥120 words unique), the working tool, 3-step HowTo,
   4+ FAQs, JSON-LD (SoftwareApp+Breadcrumb+FAQ+HowTo), related links,
   hreflang across all live languages, sitemap + IndexNow.
2. **Languages as multipliers** — every proven page family × RU/UZ today,
   then ES→PT→ID→TR→HI→AR→DE→FR (order = volume ÷ competition). Yandex
   already proves the RU thesis (68% of referral traffic).
3. **Daily content** — blog autopilot (already live) aimed at working
   niches; every post pings IndexNow.
4. **Distribution loops** — Telegram bot+channel (live), embed widget,
   viral QR footer, comparison pages, directories, Product Hunt (Aug 5).
5. **AI-search (LLM-SEO)** — llms.txt, quotable definitions atop pages,
   FAQ schema, stats pages LLMs cite ("X% of QR codes…").

## Priority loop (every session)

1. Check `growth/BACKLOG.md` → take the top unblocked item.
2. Ship it completely (code → tsc → commit → push → verify live → IndexNow).
3. Update BACKLOG (mark done, insert discovered follow-ups by ROI).
4. Update progress.md (one dense line).
5. If tokens/time remain → next item. Never stop after one.

## Hard rules

- Never add YouTube downloading (AdSense death — permanent).
- Never fake traffic/backlink schemes (ban risk).
- Never thin pages: no working tool + unique copy → don't ship it.
- Site rules in CLAUDE.md apply (design language, no new deps unless
  necessary, registration points, commit style).
- Money decisions (paid tools/ads/subscriptions) → propose to owner, never
  buy autonomously.
- KPI review weekly: GSC impressions/clicks, GA4 users + tool_used +
  search_miss, Vercel Analytics — write deltas into progress.md.

## KPIs that matter (in order)

tool_used events/day → organic clicks/day → indexed pages → impressions →
ref. domains → email/TG subscribers → revenue. Vanity (raw pageviews of
own visits) doesn't count.
